Delete Your AdventureOn Account

You can request deletion of your AdventureOn account and associated personal data even if you no longer have the AdventureOn app installed.

How to request deletion

Email support@adventureonco.com with the subject line Delete AdventureOn Account.

Please include the email address or username associated with your AdventureOn account. For security, AdventureOn may ask you to verify that you control the account before completing the request.

Email AdventureOn Support

What will be deleted

After account ownership is verified and the request is approved for processing, AdventureOn will delete or de-identify account data associated with your account where appropriate, which may include:

  • Your AdventureOn account and profile.
  • Profile information and uploaded profile media.
  • Saved items, follows, notification preferences, and similar account settings.
  • User-generated content and media associated with your account where deletion is appropriate.
  • Private account information associated with bookings, events, and communications, subject to the retention exceptions below.

Information that may be retained

AdventureOn may retain limited information when reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Fraud prevention, security, abuse prevention, and enforcement.
  • Open or disputed bookings, refunds, chargebacks, claims, or other transactions.
  • Accounting, tax, legal, or regulatory obligations.
  • Records needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Where possible, retained information will be limited to what is necessary for the applicable purpose and will no longer be used for ordinary account functionality.

Need help?

Contact support@adventureonco.com with questions about account or data deletion.
